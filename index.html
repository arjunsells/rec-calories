    XP & grade rules

    Every log earns 10 XP. After midnight, a logged day within 10% of your calorie target earns 50 XP, plus 25 XP if you hit protein. S = within 10% + protein goal; A = within 10%; B = within 20%; C = outside 20%. No logs = no grade. Today is provisional.

    Streaks count consecutive days with logs. Yesterday keeps your streak alive until today ends. All progress uses your current goals and recalculates when you edit goals or delete entries. Historical logs count automatically.
